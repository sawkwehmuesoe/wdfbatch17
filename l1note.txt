 <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0" />

name="viewport"  = the visible area of the web page on a device screen 

width=device-width
    this makes the width of the page match the screen's width (the width of a phone or tablet)

initial-scale = 1.0 
    initial zoom level when the page first load
    1.0 mean no zoom - it shows the page at 100% scale
